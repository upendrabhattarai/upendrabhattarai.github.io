Upendra R. Bhattarai

उपेन्द्र राज भट्टराई

Senior Data Scientist · Computational research scientist

Center for Translational Neuroscience · Brown University

I work at the nexus of high-dimensional data integration and molecular biology, specializing in the synthesis of multi-omic layers to decode complex cellular programs. My approach leverages a deep-rooted foundation in wet-lab experimentation to inform computational rigor, allowing me to translate massive sequencing datasets into actionable biological narratives. From the nuances of neurodevelopment and oncology to the intricacies of host–parasite dynamics, I focus on building the frameworks that turn "big data" into an understanding of life's functional architecture.

What I'm working on

  • Leading bioinformatics strategy at the Center for Translational Neuroscience — driving multi-omics integration across faculty- and student-led neurodegenerative disease projects at Brown.

  • Mentoring graduate students on computational methodology — focused on rigorous experimental design, reproducible analysis, and high-standard data interpretation.

  • Standardizing high-throughput analysis pipelines and scalable storage — increasing computational efficiency and reproducibility across the CTN.

  • Translating sequencing data into clinically meaningful results — partnering with clinical and laboratory teams on translational medicine projects.
