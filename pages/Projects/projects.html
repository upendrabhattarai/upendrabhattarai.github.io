Research

Where computational meets molecular

I focus on building and applying computational and experimental genomics to study biological systems. My work spans de novo genome assembly, transcriptomics, epigenomics, and host–parasite interactions — combining data-driven discovery with robust, reproducible workflows.

Genomics

De novo genome assembly, annotation, and comparative genomics to build high-quality references and investigate genome architecture across species.

Transcriptomics

Bulk and single-cell RNA-seq for differential expression, alternative splicing, and cell-state inference — across temporal and spatial scales.

Epigenomics

DNA methylation (WGBS) and chromatin profiling (ChIP-seq, CUT&RUN, accessibility) to decode regulatory programs underlying biological complexity.

Host–parasite

Integrative analyses to understand the molecular mechanisms of host behavioural manipulation and the evolutionary dynamics of parasite–host interactions.
