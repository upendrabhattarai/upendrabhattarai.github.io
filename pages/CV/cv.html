Upendra R. Bhattarai, PhD

Senior Data Scientist · Center for Translational Neuroscience, Brown University · Providence, RI

Download PDF Email Google Scholar GitHub

Professional summary

Computational research scientist with extensive expertise in multi-omics integration and molecular biology. Proven track record in developing analytical frameworks for genome assembly and annotation, transcriptomics (single-cell and bulk RNA-seq), and epigenetics across diverse model organisms. Expert in leveraging short and long read sequencing (PacBio / Nanopore) to investigate the molecular basis of neurodevelopmental disorders, oncology, developmental biology, and host–parasite dynamics. Skilled at leading cross-institutional and international research collaborations, and translating complex high-dimensional data into actionable biological insights through a unique blend of computational rigor and wet-lab experience.

Professional experience

Senior Data Scientist
Nov 2025 — Present
Center for Translational Neuroscience · Brown University, Providence, RI

Lead bioinformatics strategy and advance multi-omics data integration for neurodegenerative disease research, driving insights across diverse faculty- and student-led projects. Mentor and supervise graduate students on computational methodology, ensuring rigorous experimental design, reproducible analysis, and high-standard data interpretation. Collaborate with clinical and laboratory teams to translate complex sequencing data into biologically meaningful results for translational medicine. Optimise scalable data storage solutions and standardise high-throughput analysis pipelines, increasing computational efficiency and reproducibility across the CTN.

Research Associate
Mar 2024 — Nov 2025
Harvard Chan Bioinformatics Core · Department of Biostatistics, Harvard T.H. Chan School of Public Health, Boston, MA

Provided bioinformatics consultation and technical support to researchers across Harvard. Developed and delivered training workshops on bioinformatics tools, data analysis, and best practices. Collaborated with interdisciplinary teams to analyse and interpret high-throughput sequencing and omics data, and helped optimise computational workflows for large-scale biological datasets — across human, mouse, and diverse organism models.

Postdoctoral Fellow
May 2022 — Mar 2024
Extavour Lab · Department of Organismic and Evolutionary Biology, Harvard University, Cambridge, MA

Developed five hemimetabolous insect species as genetic model organisms. Assembled chromosome-level genomes, annotated, and carried out comparative genomic and transcriptomic analyses to identify evolutionary patterns in gene regulation across temporal and spatial scales.

Research Technician

Performed laboratory experiments across three trophic levels (fungus, mites, plants). Maintained cultures of acaropathogenic fungi, mites, flies, and plants, and conducted experiments on host–pathogen interactions.
